About Us

Centralrift Fresh Produce Kenya Limited is a modern agricultural company that grows, harvests, and distributes high-quality food. Based in Kenya's rich Rift Valley, we are dedicated to sustainable and environmentally friendly techniques that promote soil health, water conservation, and biodiversity.

Our agricultural professionals employ conventional and new farming methods to create fresh, organic, and healthy crops for domestic and worldwide markets.

Our product line currently comprises a wide range of fruits and vegetables and poultry husbandry, concentrating on egg production. This allows us to serve a wide range of customers, from local grocery shops and markets to international export partners, while guaranteeing that every product satisfies the highest quality requirements.
